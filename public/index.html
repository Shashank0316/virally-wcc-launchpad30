01 / THE ENGINE

Not one AI opinion.
A room full of them.

Virally models audience diversity on purpose. A trend obsessed early adopter should not react like a price sensitive buyer, and neither should sound like a generic language model.

Each agent receives the same creative but evaluates it through its own utility, novelty, trust, social and visual preferences. The system records what each one would most likely do next.

05 / Trust & method

A forecast you can interrogate.

Virally is designed to expose assumptions instead of hiding them behind one magic score.

MODEL

Persona weighted scoring

Each agent has explicit traits for novelty, social behavior, price sensitivity, trust, utility and visual responsiveness. The same creative can produce very different reactions across the room.

Signal = persona fit × creative fit × trend momentum

RESPONSIBLE USE

Human decides.

The output is a directional simulation, not a substitute for user interviews, experiments or real analytics. Keep humans in the decision loop and validate before launch spend.
